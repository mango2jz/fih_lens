1. **While reading or coding:** capture rough notes in `Inbox`; don’t interrupt every thought to organize it.
2. **At the end of the session:** turn the important items into concepts, questions, or experiments and link them to FIH Lens.
3. **Once a week:** answer a few review questions with the notes closed, then reopen them and correct your explanation.

Use `learning`, `practiced`, and `revisit` as concept statuses. A useful threshold for `practiced` is: **I can explain it, predict a small example, and identify where I used it.**
